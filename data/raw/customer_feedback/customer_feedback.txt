Customer Feedback Consolidated Summary

Over the last quarter, customers consistently reported difficulties during the onboarding and account verification process. The most common concerns included delayed verification timelines, repeated document upload requests, and lack of clarity regarding compliance requirements.

New investors often expressed uncertainty when selecting investment products and requested educational content that explained mutual funds, SIPs, risk categories, and tax implications in simple language.

Positive feedback was concentrated around the investment dashboard, portfolio tracking capabilities, SIP recommendations, and performance analytics. Many users highlighted that once onboarding was completed, the overall investing experience was intuitive and valuable.

Recommendations from customer feedback include reducing onboarding complexity, improving verification transparency, providing onboarding guidance videos, and expanding educational resources for beginner investors.
